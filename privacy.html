One Short Golf

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

One Short Golf is operated by Miguel Navarro ("we" or "us"). This policy explains what information the One Short Golf iPhone app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your Sign in with Apple identifier and the email address Apple shares with us, which may be a private relay address.
  • Profile details: your name, handicap, home area, and, if you add them, your age and bio.
  • Golf activity: tee times you post, including course, date, time, players needed, price, and round details, along with your swipes, requests, approvals, and round history.
  • Messages: chats you send to other players.
  • Safety records: reports you send or receive, players you block, moderation decisions, and any message content included with a report.
  • Device details: a push notification token if you turn on notifications.
  • Subscription details: your One Short Plus status, plan, and renewal or expiration date. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your card details.
  • Age signal: where the law requires it, Apple may tell the app whether you meet the 18+ requirement. We do not receive your birth date from Apple.

The app does not collect your precise location. Your home area and the courses you choose are selected by you.

How we use information

  • To create your account and show your profile and tee times to other players.
  • To match players, deliver chats, and send notifications.
  • To provide One Short Plus and confirm your subscription.
  • To enforce our Terms of Use, review reports, prevent abuse, and keep banned players from returning.
  • To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

What other players can see

Other players can see your profile and the tee times you post. Players in a chat with you can see the messages you send there.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. We share information only with:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database, sign-in, and server functions.
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, push notifications, age signals, and subscriptions.
  • Expo, which helps deliver push notifications.
  • RevenueCat, which manages subscription status.
  • Legal or safety recipients, when required by law or needed to protect people from harm.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. Chats are removed after they close, which happens at the end of the day after the tee time. When you delete your account, your profile, sign-in, tee times, requests, round history, chats, alerts, and the reports you sent are deleted right away. If an account was banned, a scrambled record of its sign-in identifiers is kept to prevent it from returning. Limited records may also be kept when the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Turn off notifications in your iPhone settings.
  • Block players or report them in the app.
  • Delete your account under Profile > Delete account. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, which you manage in your Apple Account settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to appeal a decision about your request. Email us to make a request.

Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Adults only

One Short Golf is only for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when this policy changes and revise the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will let you know in the app.
